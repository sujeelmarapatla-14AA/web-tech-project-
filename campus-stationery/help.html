How-to-Order Guide & Student Help Center

How Campus Stationery Ordering Works

A fast 4-step workflow to reserve your academic supplies and avoid counter queues

1
Browse & Search

Explore our catalog across writing pens, record books, drawing mini drafters, and exam kits. Filter by department or price limit.

2
Add to Cart & Apply Coupon

Select your required quantities. Enter student discount code CAMPUS10 or EXAM15 to apply subsidized savings.

3
Quick Student Checkout

Enter your name, Roll Number, department, and choose your preferred campus pickup counter. Select Cash on Collection or UPI demo.

4
Track & Collect

Receive your unique Order ID. Track live status using the order milestone stepper. Collect at Counter 1 within 10 minutes.
