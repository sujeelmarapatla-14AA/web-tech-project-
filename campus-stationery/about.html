About Campus Stationery Store

Our Academic Mission

Serving College Students with Certified Stationery Since 2012

CampusStationery is the centralized academic supply facility established to provide undergraduate and postgraduate students with high-quality, syllabus-prescribed study materials, lab records, engineering equipment, and examination kits at subsidized rates.

Located right on the ground floor of the Central Academic Block, our twin counters eliminate long queues and save student time during busy laboratory hours and university exam periods.

Our Core Commitments to Campus Students

Designed strictly around university academic guidelines and student convenience

Strict University Compliance

All drawing mini drafters, A2 imperial cartridge sheets, practical observation books, and Casio fx-991CW calculators strictly adhere to the prescribed university examination standards.

Non-Profit Subsidized Pricing

Operating under the College Student Welfare Council, stationery supplies are provided with zero retail profit markup, reducing student expenses by 15% to 25%.

Eco-Friendly Paper Initiative

Our spiral notebooks and examination answer sheets utilize 100% elemental chlorine-free (ECF) pulp sourced from sustainable certified agro-forestry plantations.
